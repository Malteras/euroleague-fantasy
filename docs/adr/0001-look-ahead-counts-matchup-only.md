# The look-ahead counts only the next round's matchup

When Proposal 2 judges a trade, it adds half of the next round's matchup bonus. It does not add half of the player's whole next-round projection. Counting the whole projection would count a strong player's quality 1.5 times. That would quietly tilt the "What matters more" balance toward points, because the price rise still covers this round only. The look-ahead is meant to judge the schedule, not the player.

## The matchup bonus is a share of his own projection

Matchup bonus = his projection × how much more (or less) PIR that opponent allows to his position than an average team × ½ (the defence numbers come from only a few games). The same rule applies this round and next round. It used to be a flat 0.12 points per percent, which gave a player who never plays the same +4.2 against an easy defence as a 20-point starter. Changed on 19 Oct 2026, the same day the look-ahead was built.

## Consequences

- Price rise, captain and lineup stay this round only. The look-ahead affects only which trades are picked.
- Coaches are the exception: a coach's projection is his opponent (a win chance), so coaches count half of their next-round projection, in both proposals.
- The look-ahead stops at the next round. Things change too much after that, and you can trade again before it.
