# The look-ahead counts half of the next round's full projection

When Proposal 2 judges a trade, it adds half of each player's next-round projection: his quality, adjusted for next round's opponent. So a strong player (Lessort) counts for more than a backup with the same easy opponent, and a player who never plays counts for nothing.

We first counted only the next round's matchup bonus, so a player's quality would not count 1.5 times. Ognjen rejected that on 19 Oct 2026: it gave every player at a club the same next-round number, whatever his level. Counting quality 1.5 times is accepted. It also means Proposal 2 leans a little more toward points than the "What matters more" setting suggests, because the price rise still covers this round only.

## The matchup bonus is a share of his own projection

Matchup bonus = his projection × how much more (or less) PIR that opponent allows to his position than an average team × ½ (the defence numbers come from only a few games). The same rule applies this round and next round. It used to be a flat 0.12 points per percent, which gave a player who never plays the same +4.2 against an easy defence as a 20-point starter.

## Consequences

- Price rise, captain and lineup stay this round only. The look-ahead affects only which trades are picked.
- Coaches count half of their next-round projection (a win chance), in both proposals. Proposal 1 players have no look-ahead.
- Out this round counts as out next round too: zero for both.
- The look-ahead stops at the next round. Things change too much after that, and you can trade again before it.
