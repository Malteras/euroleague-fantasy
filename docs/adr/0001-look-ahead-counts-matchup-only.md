# The look-ahead counts only the next round's matchup

When Proposal 2 judges a trade, it adds half of the next round's matchup bonus (0.12 × the PIR the next opponent allows to his position, × 0.5). It does not add half of the player's whole next-round projection. Counting the whole projection would count a strong player's quality 1.5 times. That would quietly tilt the "What matters more" balance toward points, because the price rise still covers this round only. The look-ahead is meant to judge the schedule, not the player.

## Consequences

- Price rise, captain and lineup stay this round only. The look-ahead affects only which trades are picked.
- Coaches are the exception: a coach's projection is its opponent (a win chance), so coaches count half of their next-round projection, in both proposals.
- The look-ahead stops at the next round. Things change too much after that, and you can trade again before it.
